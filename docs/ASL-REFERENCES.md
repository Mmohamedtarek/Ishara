# ASL references for the avatar and the glove recordings

One reference per sign, in the standard one-handed ASL form of `docs/Ishara-Sign-Vocabulary-v2-ASL.pdf` (v2.1).
The avatar animations (app Step 5) and the glove recordings (AI team) both follow these references.

- **Link:** a video page (Lifeprint, Handspeak, …) or a team video on Google Drive (shared as "anyone with the link can view"). Don't put video files in git.
- **Notes:** start time in the video, which hand, anything that differs from the vocabulary PDF.
- **Checked by:** who compared the video with the vocabulary PDF (v2.2).
