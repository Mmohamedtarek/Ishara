User decisions use the IDs from `PHASE0-REPORT.md` §7 (answered 2026-10-08, after kit v3.2.1, commit `1bbd09d`). "Draft" means: show the text or screenshots to the user before it ships.

## Release blockers
| ID | Blocker |
|---|---|
| **RB-1** (Q-A1) | The Nour / Sara avatar models are "sample models, for non-commercial use". Fine for development and the graduation demo. **They must be replaced before any store release.** Avatar files stay swappable: the character list is data, and nothing in the code depends on the model files. |
| RB-1 known issues (sample models, 2026-10-10) | Accepted by the user until the characters are replaced: (1) Sara's arm passes through her hair in CALL; the engine has no per-character hand position adjustment. (2) Sara's flat-O (HOME) stays slightly open (fingertips about 2 cm above the thumb): her thumb bones can't reach the fingertips with the shared shape; a per-character shape override was tried and didn't close it, so it was removed. Nour, the default, is correct. |
| RB-1 Sara hidden (user decision 2026-10-10) | Nour is the only avatar in v1 until a fix or the replacement characters (RB-1). One flag: `"hidden": true` on Sara in `assets/avatar/cast.json`. Hidden characters are left out of `AvatarCast.characters` (so out of every picker: Setup, Settings, Avatar studio), `showPicker` is false with one character (Nour shown alone, no empty choice), a stored or synced "sara" resolves to Nour (`byId`, masculine texts), and the engine never offers, loads or prefetches her; the avatar controller resolves ids through the cast too, so it never reports her. A hidden entry is still fully checked, so removing the flag can't break the app. Her model, strings (feminine ICU branches), code paths and tests stay; removing the flag brings her back (test). |
